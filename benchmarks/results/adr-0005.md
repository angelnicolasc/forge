# Rollout capacity under load

Forge can read the remaining budget of a rollout with 10,000 closed roots and three open roots in **0.067–0.102 ms** with the opt-in projection, down from **51.66–52.19 ms** with the canonical ledger calculation. These are median warm query times across three fresh fixture builds. The end-to-end trial reached its queue cutoff in every run. **Sustained 60 tasks/s remains on hold.** [Design decision](../../docs/decisions/0005-rollout-capacity.md) · [Machine-readable results](adr-0005.json)

## F3 · Where the time went

The query comparison held the wheel and schema constant. Each arm used Forge Check `0.4.1.dev2` on the migrated fixture. One invoked the canonical calculator and the other read the projection. The experiment varied the placement of closed history and the number of open roots and attempts. Each of 15 fixture configurations had three rebuilds, three warmups and 31 measured pairs per rebuild. The query rows below show the range of the three per-rebuild statistics for 10,000 closed roots in the active rollout.

| Measurement | Canonical | Projection |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Warm capacity median | 51.66–52.19 ms | 0.067–0.102 ms |
| Warm capacity p95 | 52.59–52.86 ms | 0.109–0.148 ms |
| First admission p95 | 67.08 ms | 5.78 ms |
| Status p95 | 116.95 ms | 46.47 ms |
| First cutoff p95 | 139.96 ms | 64.56 ms |
| Root close p95 | 3.97 ms | 3.60 ms |
| Charge recognition p95 | 3.68 ms | 4.46 ms |

The five operator rows compare the installed reference wheel `0.4.1.dev1` on schema v2 with `0.4.1.dev2` using the projection on a migrated copy. Each operator had 20 independent samples per arm. For the first cutoff, a second writer held the lock for 15 ms. Charge recognition cost an additional 0.79 ms at p95 using the unrounded samples. The separate accounting-equivalence run passed all eight cases. [Benchmark instructions](../README.md)

## F4 · What happened at 60 offered tasks/s

The end-to-end arms used the same two installed wheels as the operator comparison. A single Linux host ran Python 3.12.16, SQLite 3.40.1 in WAL mode, 12 workers and a loopback HTTP destination with its own journal. The CPU quota was 15.36 cores. The trials used separate seeded fixtures, 10,000 closed roots in the active rollout, a 50% target canary cohort, four canary slots and a nominal 5 Hz status poll. Paired arms ran serially with alternating order across three seeds. The workload was synthetic.

Each arm planned 36,000 arrivals over 600 seconds. Admission stopped when scheduled arrivals exceeded terminal tasks by more than 600 at two consecutive 30-second checks. Workers then had up to 60 seconds to drain. The table keeps scheduled, emitted and correct work distinct. Pending counts are measured at the end of the drain window.

| Trial | Arm | Cutoff | Scheduled by cutoff | Emitted | Terminal | Correct | Pending | Time after cutoff¹ |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | Canonical | 60 s | 3,601 | 1,612 | 1,265 | 1,243 | 347 | 60 s (deadline) |
| 1 | Projection | 90 s | 5,401 | 5,208 | 5,208 | 5,208 | 0 | 23.33 s |
| 2 | Canonical | 60 s | 3,601 | 1,560 | 1,102 | 1,067 | 458 | 60 s (deadline) |
| 2 | Projection | 90 s | 5,401 | 5,080 | 5,080 | 5,080 | 0 | 24.14 s |
| 3 | Canonical | 60 s | 3,601 | 1,511 | 1,116 | 1,088 | 395 | 60 s (deadline) |
| 3 | Projection | 90 s | 5,401 | 5,208 | 5,208 | 5,208 | 0 | 22.42 s |

¹ Elapsed wall time minus the scheduled cutoff. Each candidate's emitted work drained and reconciled with the destination. The early cutoff censored the planned population in both arms. Full-population p95 and p99 and a sustained throughput improvement cannot be estimated from these trials.

The qualifying run required 36,000 correct tasks, at least 59.4 useful completions/s, backlog slope ≤0.1 tasks/s, end-to-end p95 ≤2 s, p99 ≤5 s, drain ≤5 s and passing accounting and dispatch checks. Calibration at 12 and 40 offered tasks/s met the admission and close p95 limits (1.15 × reference p95 + 10 ms). Under saturation the candidate's status read p95 reached **189–196 ms** across the three trials, above the 100 ms status target. A separate 60-second run with minimal instrumentation completed 3,600/3,600 candidate tasks with end-to-end p95 of **20.81 s**.

For the slowest 5% of completed candidate roots in each primary trial, mean time waiting for a worker was **22.40–23.73 s**. On those same roots, mean cumulative writer acquisition took **256–296 ms** and capacity work nested inside the writer took **0.63–0.70 ms**. The next performance experiment belongs at the worker queue. End-to-end results apply to the two installed configurations above. The dev2 default still uses the canonical calculator.

## Provenance

The [JSON extract](adr-0005.json) records the SHA-256 of each original receipt and protocol, both wheel digests, and the measured, qualified and final revisions. The candidate wheel was byte-identical across those revisions. Each key under `fields` is a JSON Pointer into its source file. Query and operator durations retain their original nanoseconds, load metrics retain their source units. The digest names the full source bytes, including fields omitted from the extract.
